The difference

Most form plugins send an email and forget the lead. RivetForm keeps it.

01

Half finished entries are saved

If a visitor types their email or phone and then leaves without sending, the entry is still kept and marked abandoned, so the lead is not lost.

02

Duplicates are caught

Phone numbers match on their last 10 digits and emails match case insensitively, so the same person is recognised across formats. Flag, reject, or link to the existing contact.

03

Every lead exports in one click

A lead inbox with stages, owners and follow up reminders, and a one click CSV export of every submission with its duplicate flags.

The builder

Drag fields in, watch the form build live.

17 field types, from plain text to phone with a country picker, address with autocomplete, file upload, and a quiz field that doubles as a spam check. Conditional logic shows or hides fields, and it is enforced on the server too, so a hidden field cannot be faked through.

Everything, included free

One plugin, the whole pipeline.

Lead inbox

Every submission gets a stage (New, Contacted, Qualified, Won, Lost), an owner, a follow up date with an email reminder, and an activity history. Filter by stage, owner or follow ups due.

Stored first, always

Each submission is saved to its own database table before any email or webhook runs. If a notification fails, the entry is still there with the exact reason.

WhatsApp and SMS alerts

Free WhatsApp through CallMeBot, SMS or WhatsApp through Twilio with approved templates, or the official Meta Cloud API. All three built and verified.

Webhooks

Send any form to Zapier, Make, n8n or a custom endpoint. Failed deliveries retry up to 4 times with backoff, and every attempt is logged.

Stripe payments

Turn on payment for a form and the visitor goes to a Stripe hosted checkout. No card fields are built or stored on the site.

Spam protection, on by default

Honeypot, a signed time check and a per IP rate limit all run out of the box. Optional reCAPTCHA or Cloudflare Turnstile, matched against WordPress's own disallowed keywords, with a quiz field as a no tracking alternative.

Multistep forms

Split a form into steps with a progress bar and per step validation, or switch to one question per screen.

Notifications with smart tags

Unlimited notifications per form, each with its own To, From, Reply To, CC, BCC, merge tags and a conditional send rule.

Contact Form 7 importer

Finds existing CF7 forms and imports their fields, mail settings and success messages. The original CF7 forms are left untouched.

Cookieless analytics

Views, starts, submissions, conversion rate and a step funnel per form. No visitor tracking, no cookies.

Privacy and accessibility

Hooks into WordPress's own export and erase tools by email. Validation errors appear under the right field, focus moves there, and screen readers announce it.

How it was verified

Tested like it ships to thousands.

Delivery is the part that fails quietly, so I built a sandbox that intercepts outgoing calls and simulates Twilio, Meta and CallMeBot against their real documented rules: error codes, template approval states and rate limits.

  • 32 of 32 automated cases pass, covering SMS, WhatsApp free text, templates, malformed input, wrong credentials and unverified recipients
  • Real errors checked against the live services with fake credentials, so parsing matches reality, not just the sandbox
  • Full pipeline run with real test leads: storage, lead inbox, duplicate flag, CSV export and delivery logging
  • Plugin Check, the official WordPress.org linting tool, passes clean
