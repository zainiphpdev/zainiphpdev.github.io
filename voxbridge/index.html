What it does

Everything your team needs around a call

No more switching between your phone and your CRM, and no more calls that never get logged.

☎

Click-to-call

Dial any phone number from Accounts, Contacts, Leads, and custom modules, across detail views, list views, and subpanels.

◱

Live call popup

A floating card shows the matched caller with a timer, a disposition, and a notes box that saves straight to the call record.

✎

Automatic logging

Direction, status, duration, disposition, recording link, and the related record are captured with zero typing.

⇄

Two ways to call

Ring your Asterisk desk extensions over AMI, or place and receive calls in the browser with the built-in WebRTC softphone.

⌕

Smart caller lookup

Inbound and outbound numbers are matched against every phone field in your instance and linked to the right record.

●

Call recordings

Each call record carries a link to the recording your PBX produced, so the whole conversation is one click away.

How it works

Live in three steps

STEP 01

Install

Upload the package in Module Loader, accept the licence, and run Quick Repair. The module, tables, fields, and admin screens are added in one step.

STEP 02

Configure

Add your PBX server and WebRTC settings on one admin screen, then set each user's extension and softphone.

STEP 03

Call

Click any number to dial. The popup identifies the caller, and the call logs itself to the right record.

In the record

See who is calling, log it in seconds

On every outbound and inbound call, a draggable popup resolves the caller across all your modules. Pick a disposition, add a note, and save. It never blocks the page behind it.

For admins

One screen to connect your phone system

Add Asterisk/FreePBX servers for desk-phone dialling and your WSS gateway for the browser softphone. Per-user extensions and softphone credentials live on each user's profile.
